# Buyer panel: גלריית הקסם (The Magic Gallery), a Hebrew phone-magic app

**28 buy · 72 pass** (28% buy) out of 100 simulated buyers. Seed 895392, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- If it were sold through the App Store or Google Play with real reviews, and the free tricks actually fooled my family at dinner.
- Real videos of regular Israeli people with no magic background pulling off the trick, plus reviews I can trust. Trying the free tricks first and seeing that they actually work and are easy for me would help too.
- Real unedited videos of regular people (not magicians) doing the tricks on friends, plus Israeli user reviews and a clear name or company behind it, with a refund if it doesn't work on my phone.
- Being able to buy it through the App Store or Google Play, or paying with Apple Pay/Bit, so I'm not typing my card into an unknown site.
- If I could buy it through the App Store or Google Play, or pay with PayPal/Bit so my card never touches their site.
- A short video of a regular person like me pulling it off at a party, and the free tricks working for me on the first try so I know I won't fail.
- If it were sold through the App Store or Google Play, so I pay the way I always do and can get a refund if it doesn't work.
- If the free tricks absolutely blew the kids away and the full version was a one-time 20-30 ₪, I might break my rule once.
- If the free tricks actually fooled my family on Friday and I saw real Israeli magicians vouching for it, I might pay, and only with a refund guarantee or through Bit/PayPal instead of my card.
- If the free tricks actually worked on my kids and friends without me fumbling, and someone in my WhatsApp group vouched for it, I might pay a much lower price, something around 30.
- A party or birthday coming up and seeing a free trick actually fool someone after five minutes of setup.
- If the free tricks worked so well that my friends freaked out and I wanted more, and the full version cost around 30 shekels.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.

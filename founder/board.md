# Board summary: גלריית הקסם

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, 0 PASS. Average score 4.3 / 10** (Offers 4, Monopoly 4, Product 5).

## Risks more than one member raised (first)
1. **No proven distribution** (Offers, Monopoly). 100,000 ₪ a month = about 1,010 new one-time buyers a month, with no sales, no channel and no cost per customer known yet.
2. **Visible seams break the "never suspicious" promise** (all three). The spectator sees sccs1298.github.io; the free ntfy relay (about 250 messages a day per IP) can fail mid-show.
3. **No single buyer and no focus** (all three). "Anyone" or "magicians", Israel or worldwide, and many tricks with no one hero trick.
4. **High effort for a first-time buyer** (Offers, Product). Secret inputs, phone pairing and watch setup need practice; no guided first run.
5. **Easy to copy / not unique enough** (Monopoly, Product). The Wikipedia trick resembles an existing product; the web app cannot control the real phone.

## Conditions, merged (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] Pick one buyer (the board leans to Hebrew-speaking hobby and pro magicians first) and one market (Israel first).
- [ ] Pick one hero trick (the Gallery is the natural candidate) and lead with it.
- [ ] Buy a short own domain before selling; remove the github.io address from everything the spectator sees.
- [ ] A relay that will not fail during a show (paid ntfy tier or own server), or a clear fallback.
- [ ] Finish the purchase path: live payment, automatic license delivery.
- [ ] A guided first run: the buyer performs one trick well within about 5 minutes.
- [ ] Proof before marketing: real performance videos and the first paying buyers.
- [ ] A guarantee the margin can afford (CFO).
- [ ] Map competitors and their prices before judging 99 ₪ (/founder-competitors).
- [ ] Measure cost per customer on one channel before expanding worldwide (/founder-cfo, /founder-marketing).
- [ ] Name the moat: brand in the Israeli magic community, content, or a method that is truly ahead.

## The strongest version the board can see
Not quite what was pitched. Instead of "the best magic app in the world in every parameter, for everyone", the board sees **the first Hebrew-native phone-magic app for Israeli hobby and pro magicians, led by one flawless hero trick (the Gallery), on its own short domain, with a 5-minute guided first performance and real performance videos as proof.** Win that small market, build a name inside the Israeli magic community, measure what a customer costs, and only then add the spectator-phone tricks as upgrades and go wider. The members disagree on how much the extra tricks help: the Offers lens sees them as a valuable stack, the Product lens sees them as a lack of focus until the hero trick is flawless.

גלריית הקסם: an app in Hebrew that lets you read minds with your own phone.
Your friend thinks of any word or number, and it appears inside a video in your phone's Photos gallery, in an old locked note, in your calculator, or as an incoming call. They can even search on their own phone, and you secretly know what they searched.
Works on iPhone and Android, installed from the browser (not the App Store), works offline. A few tricks are free to try.
Full version: 99 ₪, one payment, lifetime access. Paid by card on the app's website.
